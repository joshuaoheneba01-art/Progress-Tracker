STICK-TO-IT TRACKER (standalone app)

Files in this folder: index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, and 4 icon PNGs.
Keep them all together in the same folder.

PUT IT ONLINE FREE (GitHub Pages)
1. On github.com click New repository. Name it mcjayy-tracker. Set it to Public. Create it.
2. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in ALL the files from this folder (not the zip itself), then Commit.
3. Go to Settings > Pages. Under Branch pick main and / (root). Save.
4. Wait about a minute. Your app is at https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mcjayy-tracker/

Alternative with no GitHub: drag this folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop

INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE
Android (Chrome): open the link, menu > Install app.
iPhone (Safari): open the link, Share > Add to Home Screen.

TURN ON ALERTS
Open the app > Settings.
1. Download calendar file, open it, and let your calendar import it. Alerts fire 10 minutes before every block, even when the app is closed.
2. Turn on reminders for extra in-app nudges (these work while the app is open or recently used).

YOUR DATA
Progress is saved on your phone only. It is not uploaded anywhere.
Use Settings > Export now and then to keep a backup.
